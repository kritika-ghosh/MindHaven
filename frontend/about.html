I. Core Multimodal ML Model

MindHaven features a state-of-the-art CatBoost Regressor framework designed to evaluate mental fatigue objectively. Instead of relying solely on subjective forms, the engine evaluates stress using three distinct channels:

  • Psychometrics: Short survey baseline modeled after the Maslach Burnout Inventory.
  • Computer Vision: Computes Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) for blinking fatigue and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) for muscular tension.
  • Vocal Sentiment: Utilizes speech recognition and VADER sentiment dictionaries to detect tone stability.

II. Real-Time Supportive Therapy Chatbot

Our interactive therapist chatbot, located at the bottom-right of the interface, acts as an on-demand mental health companion. It processes user chat sentiment in real-time, matching response styling and therapeutic guidance directly to your current stress profile. The chatbot suggests coping strategies, guides users through grounding techniques, and tracks verbal sentiment progress over time.

III. Explainable Predictive Graph

Your mental state shouldn't be a black box. MindHaven provides a personalized explanation graph based on SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values to highlight what is driving your fatigue:

  • Coral Red (Right-pulling): Symptoms or biometric anomalies (like high yawning rates or sleep deficits) raising your stress rating.
  • Sage Green (Left-pulling): Protective factors (like active workouts or social breaks) keeping your rating down.
air

IV. Therapeutic Breathing (Pranayama)

To help users immediately de-escalate acute stress, the platform integrates paced breathing modules. The breathing guide helps synchronize heart rate variability and settle autonomic nervous tension. Users can practice daily, which lowers facial muscular markers (MAR) and feeds positive signals back into the diagnostic model.
